Purpose

Capture what a customer wants to buy and turn it into a confirmed order. Sales owns the journey from an open cart to a placed, confirmed order, serving the Customer (who buys) and indirectly the Merchant (whose catalog is sold). evidence: docs/architecture-communication-canvas.md - Core Functions
